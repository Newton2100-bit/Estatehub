The Individual, Professional, and Enterprise plans previously shown here were design placeholders. They are not available subscriptions, and EstateHub does not currently charge a monthly platform fee.

What you can do in this prototype

  • Landlords can submit properties and manage maintenance jobs after account approval.
  • Tenants can browse listings, submit maintenance requests, and view their account records.
  • Workers can browse jobs, request approval, update assigned work, and see payout status.

Rent collection and worker payout features support Safaricom Daraja when the business has configured its M-Pesa account and public callback service. External transfers can also be recorded in the ledger.
